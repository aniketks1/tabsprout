Domain Clustering

Instant hostname extraction with brand dictionaries (e.g. GitHub, Google Docs, Notion, YouTube) and intelligent subdomain grouping.

Smart Topic Heuristics

Clusters Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo queries by research theme and extracts dominant keywords from page titles without external AI APIs.

Custom Project Rules

Create custom workspaces (e.g. "Sprint 42", "Client Alpha") with wildcard match patterns (*jira*, *github*) and custom colors.

100% On-Device Privacy

Three ways to organize your workflow

Pick the strategy that matches how your brain works today.

Deterministic & Instant

Extracts root domains, trims www., cleans up complex TLDs (co.uk, com.au), and maps them into branded titles like GitHub, Google Drive, or Linear.

github.com/facebook/react + github.com/vitejs/vite → GitHub (2)

Smart Search-Intent Heuristics

Recognizes research queries on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo and groups matching search queries together with shared title keywords.

google.com/search?q=react+hooks + react.dev/reference → React Hooks (2)

Custom Priority Match Rules

Configure your own workspaces with wildcard patterns or regular expressions. Tabs matching your rules are automatically filed into the right group.

Patterns: *jira*, *figma*, *confluence* → Sprint Q4 (3)
